About

A Geneva atelier,
built on patience.

Founded in 1987 in a small workshop above the Rhône, AURELIS has always made fewer watches than it could — so that each one can be made properly.

Every case is machined and polished in rose gold, every movement is assembled by a single watchmaker, and every watch is regulated for fifteen days before it leaves Geneva.
